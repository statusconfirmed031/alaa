In the heart of the GCC region, WMINFRA Interior finishing contractors was born, driven by a passion for interior craftsmanship and a commitment to quality.

Our journey began with a focus on delivering exceptional spaces that exceed expectations.

OUR HISTORY

2006Dubai, UAE

Built on expertise.
Driven by craftsmanship.

WMINFRA Interior finishing contractors was founded in 2006 in Dubai, UAE, by industry experts Mr. Ahmed Abdallah Al Shafar and Mr. Talal Saeed. With a strong background in Hospitality, Fit-out, Joinery, Metal, Marble, and FF&E, they established WMINFRA Interior finishing contractors as a premier interior contracting and manufacturing business.
