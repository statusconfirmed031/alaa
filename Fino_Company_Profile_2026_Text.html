Our Company

At WMINFRA Interior finishing contractors, we promise to:

In the heart of the GCC region, WMINFRA Interior finishing contractors was born, driven by a passion for interior craftsmanship and a commitment to quality.

Our journey began with a focus on delivering exceptional spaces that exceed expectations.

Our clients trust us to bring their visions to life. We’ve earned a reputation for quality, reliability, and attention to detail.

The repeat business we receive, along with industry certifications, serves as a testament to our unwavering commitment.

We’ve had the privilege of working on diverse projects, from luxurious villas and hotels to corporate offices, airports, and retail spaces, among others.

Each project has taught us something new, and we’ve honed our skills in joinery, woodwork, metal, and marble craftsmanship.

Over 20 years, we’ve grown into a leading ISO certified interior fit-out contractor.

OUR HISTORY

2006Dubai, UAE

Built on expertise.
Driven by craftsmanship.

WMINFRA Interior finishing contractors was founded in 2006 in Dubai, UAE, by industry experts Mr. Ahmed Abdallah Al Shafar and Mr. Talal Saeed. With a strong background in Hospitality, Fit-out, Joinery, Metal, Marble, and FF&E, they established WMINFRA Interior finishing contractors as a premier interior contracting and manufacturing business.

Our group companies work together seamlessly, offering:

Turnkey solutionsIn-house design, development, engineering, and project executionDiverse and talented staffMulti-skilled workforce
