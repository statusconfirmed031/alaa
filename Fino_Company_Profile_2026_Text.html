Our Company
At Fino International, we promise to:

• Deliver exceptional quality and craftsmanship.
• Listen to your needs and exceed your expectations.
• Build lasting relationships through transparency and trust.

In the heart of the GCC region, Fino International was born, driven by a passion for interior
craftsmanship and a commitment to quality.

Our journey began with a focus on delivering exceptional spaces that exceed expectations.

Our clients trust us to bring their visions to life. We’ve earned a reputation for quality, reliability, and
attention to detail.

The repeat business we receive, along with industry certifications, serves as a testament to our
unwavering commitment.

We’ve had the privilege of working on diverse projects, from luxurious villas and hotels to corporate
offices, airports, and retail spaces, among others.

Each project has taught us something new, and we’ve honed our skills in joinery, woodwork, metal,
and marble craftsmanship.

Over 20 years, we’ve grown into a leading ISO certified interior fit-out contractor.

Our History

Fino International was founded in 2006 in Dubai, UAE, by industry experts Mr.
Ahmed Abdallah Al Shafar and Mr. Talal Saeed. With a strong background in
Hospitality, Fit-out, Joinery, Metal, Marble, and FF&E, they established Fino
International as a premier interior contracting and manufacturing business.

Our group companies work together seamlessly offering:

• Turnkey solutions
• In-house design, development, engineering, and project execution
• Diverse and talented staff
• Multi-skilled workforce
